Context

The challenge

When inquiries arrive through different channels, someone has to record the lead, decide who should follow up, and remember to update its status. Manual handoffs can make it harder for a team to see which inquiries still need attention.

Approach

The solution

This demo sends new lead details into Asana as an organized task, with follow-up information and a status the team can update. The project gives the team one place to review incoming inquiries and their next steps.

Process

How it works

1

Receive a new lead

A new inquiry enters the workflow with the available contact and request details.

2

Create an Asana task

Zapier adds the lead to the selected Asana project so the team can review it.

3

Set up the follow-up

The task gives the team a place to record the owner, next action, and follow-up timing.

4

Track the lead status

As the team works the inquiry, they update its status to show what needs attention next.

Intended outcome

Make lead follow-up easier to see and manage

The workflow is designed to reduce repetitive lead entry and make follow-up tasks visible in Asana. It can help a team keep track of inquiries, while people remain responsible for reviewing each lead and updating its progress.
