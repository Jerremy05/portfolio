Context

The challenge

Reviewing resumes against job requirements and organizing follow-ups can take time. Candidate details, screening answers, and interview schedules may also be stored in different places.

Approach

The solution

The workflow collects applicant details and resumes, compares candidate information with the selected job requirements, and organizes candidate statuses. Potential candidates can receive a questionnaire and personalized follow-up, with interview scheduling coordinated through the workflow.

Process

How it works

1

Collect application

The applicant submits a form and resume; details and the file are saved for review.

2

Review resume

The workflow extracts resume information and compares it with the job description.

3

Organize candidate status

Applicant records are updated with a status to help the hiring team organize its review.

4

Follow up and schedule

Potential candidates can receive a questionnaire and personalized email, then move through screening and interview scheduling.

Outcome

Candidate reviews and follow-ups are easier to coordinate

This demo organizes applicant information, questionnaires, email follow-ups, and interview scheduling in one workflow. It helps the hiring team review candidates while leaving hiring decisions with people.
