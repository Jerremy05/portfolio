Context

The challenge

Social media questions can arrive at any time, and answering repeated inquiries manually can take staff away from other work. Responses can also vary when useful business information is spread across different documents.

Approach

The solution

The workflow filters incoming requests, retrieves relevant information from a connected knowledge source, and gives it to an AI agent to prepare a helpful response. Conversation memory helps the assistant follow the thread of a continuing conversation.

Process

How it works

1

Receive a message

A social media message reaches the workflow through its webhook.

2

Filter the request

The workflow checks the incoming request and continues along the appropriate path.

3

Retrieve helpful information

Relevant information is fetched from a connected document or knowledge source.

4

Prepare a response

The AI agent uses the retrieved information and conversation memory to prepare a reply.

Outcome

More consistent replies to common social media questions

This demo shows how incoming messages can be matched with relevant information and answered with AI support. The connected knowledge source helps ground responses, while conversation memory supports follow-up messages.
