Context

The challenge

Customers often ask the same questions about services, prices, and policies through Messenger. Finding the right answer across business documents takes staff time, and inconsistent or outdated information can make replies harder to manage.

Approach

The solution

The demo retrieves relevant passages from indexed business documents and gives them to the AI agent as context for its reply. It can collect a customer’s details and route a question to a person when the available information does not support a reliable answer.

Process

How it works

1

Prepare business documents

Service details, pricing, and policies are added as the agent’s knowledge sources.

2

Index the information

Documents are split into searchable sections and stored with their embeddings in Supabase.

3

Retrieve relevant passages

When a Messenger question arrives, the workflow searches for document sections related to it.

4

Reply or refer to a person

The agent responds using retrieved context, or asks a team member to help if it lacks reliable information.

Intended outcome

Give customers quicker access to documented answers

This demo shows how a Messenger agent can use business documents to answer common questions, collect lead details, and pass uncertain requests to staff. Retrieval helps ground the reply in available information, but it does not guarantee every answer is complete or correct. Keep the source documents current and review the agent’s behavior before using it with customers.
