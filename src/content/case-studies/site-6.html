Context

The challenge

Useful ideas from videos, interviews, and transcripts can take time to reshape for each social platform. Rewriting the same source repeatedly can slow down a consistent posting routine.

Approach

The solution

The workflow takes existing material, identifies its main ideas, and prepares a set of platform-specific post drafts. It saves the drafts for a person to review, edit, and approve before publishing.

Process

How it works

1

Provide the source

Submit a video transcript or other existing material to start the workflow.

2

Identify key ideas

The AI uses the source to find useful points, themes, and supporting details.

3

Create platform drafts

It adapts the ideas into draft posts with formats suited to selected platforms.

4

Review before publishing

A person checks accuracy, tone, and formatting, then edits or approves each draft.

Outcome

More usable drafts from material you already have

The demo shows how one video or transcript can become several starting-point posts without publishing automatically. Human review remains part of the process so the final wording can be checked and adjusted for the brand and audience.
